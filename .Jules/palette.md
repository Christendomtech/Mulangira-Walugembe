## 2025-02-14 - Skip to Content and Accessible Navigation Anchors
**Learning:** For a single-page app containing multiple main content sections, fixed navigation bars require matching page scroll offset rules (such as Tailwind's `scroll-pt-20`) to prevent anchor-scrolled titles from being obscured by the header. Furthermore, a semantic `<main>` section wrapper is essential to preserve correct document outline, and any dynamic mid-dots or separator characters (e.g. ` · `) must be programmatically split and wrapped in an `aria-hidden="true"` span to prevent screen readers from reading them out loud.
**Action:** Always add a high-contrast `.skip-to-content` link that moves focus directly to `#main-content`, ensure global nav and footer stay outside `<main>`, set corresponding `scroll-pt-[header-height]` on the `html` element, and split meta-strings programmatically to wrap separators inside `aria-hidden="true"`.
