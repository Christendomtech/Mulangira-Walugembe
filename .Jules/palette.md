## 2026-05-29 - Accessibility Standard for Portfolio Sites
**Learning:** For high-end portfolio sites (like a Royal Family member's), accessibility shouldn't just be present—it should be integrated into the design system. Standardizing the 'Skip to Content' link style and focus rings across all interactive elements ensures a polished feel for keyboard users without compromising the aesthetic for mouse users.

**Action:** Always implement a `.skip-to-content` utility in `index.css` and use a consistent `focus-visible:ring-accent` pattern on all interactive components to maintain brand consistency during keyboard navigation.
