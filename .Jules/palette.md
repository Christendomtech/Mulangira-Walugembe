## 2025-05-15 - Portfolio Accessibility Audit
**Learning:** Repetitive CTA text like "Explore" or "Request a copy" in grid-based portfolio layouts creates significant ambiguity for screen reader users when not accompanied by unique ARIA labels.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive ARIA labels (e.g., "Explore [Project Name] details") for repetitive interactive elements and ensure a "Skip to content" link is present to bypass redundant navigation.
