Three pillars

The work, in brief

A life organised around three commitments — each rooted in the others.

I.

Heritage

Stewarding the cultural inheritance of the Buganda Kingdom through respectful representation, education, and the preservation of memory.

II.

Enterprise

Building bridges between Ugandan agriculture, tourism, and global markets — coffee, trade, and investment as engines of dignified livelihoods.

III.

Service

Patronage of causes that lift the next generation: education, youth empowerment, and the quiet work of cultural diplomacy.
